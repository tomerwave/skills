# Image direction

## Principle

Do not illustrate the headline literally. Illustrate the hidden idea physically.

## Visual style

- Photorealistic editorial photography
- Minimal composition
- No visible people by default
- One focal object or anomaly
- Natural, cold, or slightly warm restrained lighting
- Real offices, hallways, journals, server rooms, city infrastructure
- No neon cyberpunk, hooded hackers, floating interfaces, robot hands, or glowing brains
- No quote cards unless explicitly requested

## Metaphor patterns

### Staying after it is over

An open journal calendar with almost every day crossed out. Slightly imperfect handwriting. One remaining day carries tension.

### Everything is hackable, but work continues

A lit office building at night with one visibly shattered window while every other floor continues operating.

### Technically public, socially hidden

A slightly open door at the end of an empty hallway. It is accessible, but clearly not an invitation.

### AI as the explanation for layoffs

An empty conference room after a meeting. One chair pulled away. A laptop remains open. A strategy slide is still visible in the background.

### AI kill switch

A realistic industrial emergency-stop button behind a transparent cover in a functioning server room.

## Prompt construction

Include:

1. medium and composition,
2. setting,
3. focal metaphor,
4. lighting and mood,
5. exclusions.

Example:

```text
Photorealistic editorial photograph of a quiet modern hallway at night, symmetrical composition, one wooden door at the far end left slightly open, warm light spilling through a narrow gap into an otherwise dark neutral corridor, no people, no text, subtle tension, realistic architecture, restrained colors, not cinematic sci-fi, no horror elements.
```

## Quality check

Before generating, verify:

- Is the metaphor legible without the caption?
- Is it subtle enough to avoid becoming a meme?
- Does it look like a real photograph rather than generic AI art?
- Are dates, calendars, labels, and written words factually correct?
- Would the image still work in a LinkedIn feed crop?
