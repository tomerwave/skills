# Voice and structure

## Voice

Write like a technically informed founder noticing something uncomfortable in public.

Characteristics:

- Direct rather than polished
- Observational rather than preachy
- Personal opinion without autobiography unless relevant
- Emotionally intelligent without sounding like therapy content
- Technically accurate without jargon dumps
- Confident, but willing to preserve uncertainty

Prefer:

- “They weren’t leaked. They were public links.”
- “There is a third privacy state now.”
- “The breaches won’t stop. They’ll just become normal again.”

Avoid:

- “In today’s rapidly evolving digital landscape…”
- “This serves as a powerful reminder…”
- “The key takeaway for leaders is…”
- overly symmetrical three-part rhetoric in every post

## Reliable structures

### Event → distinction → truth

```text
Claude shared chats were showing up on Google.

They weren’t leaked.

They were public links.

But there is a third privacy state now.
...
```

### Event → contradiction

```text
A Democrat and a Republican introduced the AI Kill Switch Act.

For years, every AI announcement sounded the same:
more capable, more autonomous, able to run longer without us.

Now the question is whether we can still turn it off.
```

### Detail → recognition → final image

```text
He kept a journal.
An X on every day the system blocked his work.

I know that journal.
Not the software one.
...
You already left. You just have not moved your things yet.
```

## Length control

- Hook: 1–3 lines
- Context: only what is needed to understand the event
- Observation: 3–8 short lines
- Ending: 1–3 lines
- Remove any paragraph that repeats the implication of the ending

## Strong endings

A strong ending:

- changes how the reader interprets the event
- is concrete or visual
- does not explain itself afterward
- can stand alone without becoming a slogan

Examples:

- “It just felt private because nobody was looking yet.”
- “The product was infrastructure to you. To the company, it was still an experiment.”
- “The decision was already made. AI was the explanation left on the screen.”
